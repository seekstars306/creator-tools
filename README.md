# Creator Tools

Fix subtitle and video chores in seconds.

**Live site: https://tools.policegold.buzz/**

Free browser-based tools for creators who work with subtitles and video:

- **SRT Converter** – convert between subtitle formats
- **Subtitle Shifter** – shift subtitle timing in bulk
- **Chapter Generator** – generate video chapters from timestamps
- **Subtitle Encoding Fixer** – fix garbled subtitles (GBK, Shift-JIS, and more)
- **Bilingual Subtitle Merger** – merge English and Chinese subtitles
- **CJK Subtitle Checker** – check CJK subtitles for common issues
- **Fix Out-of-Sync Subtitles** – resync subtitles with audio
- **Convert SRT to WebVTT** – one-click SRT → WebVTT conversion

Plus step-by-step guides for fixing out-of-sync subtitles and converting SRT to WebVTT.

## How it works

Everything runs entirely in your browser. No uploads, no sign-up, no server-side processing — your files never leave your device.

## Tech

Pure static HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No build step, no backend. Hosted as a static site.

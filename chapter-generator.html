YouTube Chapter Generator

Enter one chapter per line as time - title, for example 0:00 - Intro. The tool formats them for YouTube and checks the basic chapter rules.

YouTube chapter rules, in plain English

  • The first chapter must start at 0:00.
  • You need at least three chapters.
  • Each chapter should be at least 10 seconds long, and chapters should be in time order.
  • Put the chapter list in the video description, not only in a comment.

If the checker flags a problem, fix that line and generate again. This tool does not upload anything or change your video; it only formats text for you to paste.
